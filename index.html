About

I'm an economist, AI safety advocate and animal advocate based in Lagos. I'm the founder of The AI Guardian, an AI safety advocacy platform.

My work spans AI governance in Africa and the Global South, the welfare of animals and digital minds, resilient food systems, and the economics of how technology power is distributed. I'm building career capital to contribute to global betterment through focused, impactful work.
